Discrete indexing, coefficient arithmetic, numerical representation, and

## Corrections Required Before Promotion

- Replace "floats are uncheckable" with "floating-point claims require explicit
  rounding, tolerance/error bounds, and an appropriate oracle." IEEE values are
  finite representations; exact bitwise checks and bounded numerical checks are
  both possible. Neither replaces an exact rational certificate when that is
  the claim being made.
- Replace "Z7 makes K characteristic 7" with an explicit coefficient ring.
  Positions are cyclic modulo 7; coefficients may be integer, rational, real,
  or characteristic 7. The real spectrum and modular identity are not the same
  assertion. See the fresh convolution in `post-d5-receipt-check.md`.
- Replace "physical gamut" with the appropriate bound: geometric-mean
  wavelengths stay in the numerical A0 interval `[400,700]` nm, not a proven
  display gamut or perceptual hull. Sum-mixing is outside that interval and has
  `photonicCompression:null`, not physically impossible wavelengths.
- Preserve all four interpretation flags. The presence of physical constants
  does not turn an authored binding into a `physicalQuantityClaim`.
- Do not repeat 325.9 as a verified value. It lies outside the four documented
  derived wavelength bands; its intended formula and units remain unspecified.
- Keep the Fibonacci observation scoped/noncausal. A conditional 20 upper bound
  is not a measured terminal value, and A-ladder termination is not evidence for
  a Fibonacci mechanism.

## Scrutiny And Falsification

**Proposed check:** for each future claim, identify its domain, coefficient
ring, source input slice, computational representation, comparison semantics,
and authority boundary before evaluating it. Check the candidate distinctions
against actual records and generators, not a narrative similarity.

**Counterexample handling:** an in-scope source showing that one table row
misstates its domain, numerical semantics, or authority requires revising this
draft. A real-valued descriptor which is independently validated does not
falsify finite topology, and renderer failure is not a counterexample to the
underlying arithmetic. Reject stronger universal conclusions as out of scope.

**Verification status:** source review and the 50 focused tests recorded in
`post-d5-receipt-check.md` support the cited components, not this typology as a
new theorem. No dedicated typology validator or ledger-grade falsification
suite exists or ran. Maintainer scrutiny must settle whether a conceptual
taxonomy belongs in an observation ledger at all, whose current policy calls
for derived identities with canonical-data counterexamples
(`provenance/OBSERVATION_LEDGER.md:1-6`).

**Binding philosophy:** planning prose carries no mathematical, topology,
office, runtime, admission, or global harmonic.C_H authority. Any later
promotion requires separately reviewed source bindings, executable scope where
applicable, and an authorized record. This draft reserves no ID.

## 4. Secular theurgy: the ENTRY-8 reading, with fences

**Definition (historical, not receipted):** theurgy — late-antique Neoplatonic technical term (Iamblichus, Proclus). Distinguished from theology and ordinary ritual: the practice of making divine structure *present and operative* through exact symbolic performance. Premise: symbols are handles on hidden formal structure; correctly executed symbol-manipulation causes real effects. Participation, not representation.

**The reading (interpretive):** the project executed theurgy's core claim in the only domain where it can work. Three acts: (1) the Chaldean order adopted as authored axiom — honest convention; (2) given a formal universe — the Z₇ ring, the kernel, the windows; (3) **the theurgical moment, receipted as GOV-517: a blind engine derived D₅'s contact geometry from the construction algebra the Chaldean ring organizes.** The symbol-system produced structure its authors did not put into it; declared and derived met; the meeting was receipted (OBS-022, ENTRY 8). Symbols became *operative* — generating truths, out-deriving their authors.

**Why secular:** the power source is inverted. Traditional theurgy works by plugging into a divine cosmos; this works because the symbol-system is small, finite, fully axiomatized. Operative means *derivable*, in a closed world. The ancients wanted symbols acting on reality at large; the framework demonstrates symbols acting on a universe built for them. Same phenomenology — authored symbols acquiring consequence beyond authorship — minus the metaphysics.

**The fences (these are load-bearing; any durable statement of this reading must carry them):**
1. **One tier.** D₅ derived; D1–D4, D6–D7 untested. The theurgy is currently one theorem deep. D₄'s registration is the next test of extent.
2. **One semantics.** The derivation is bounded by G1–G5, maintainer-registered. The symbols out-derived the *D₅ declaration*, not the *semantics enabling derivation*.
3. **The canalization caveat — the honest edge:** D₅'s derivability is compatible with the authors having *canalized* — chosen the declaration the surrounding mathematics made natural. "Symbols that generate" vs. "authors who intuited" cannot be separated by any single derivation, possibly by nothing. This is H1-vs-H3 in ancient dress. The framework exists because the maintainer wants them separated or wants to know they can't be.

**Standing formulation (interpretive, offered as the durable sentence if this reading is ever registered):** *an authored symbolic order, treated as an axiom and given formal semantics, becomes genuinely operative — it generates truths beyond its authors' input — demonstrated once (D₅), bounded by registered semantics, extent unknown, with the authorship reading unfalsified.*

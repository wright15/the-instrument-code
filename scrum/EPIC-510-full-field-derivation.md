# EPIC-510 - Full-Field Derivation

**Status:** Backlog · **Priority:** High · **Owner:** Research and provenance governance
**Epic ID:** EPIC-510 · **Release:** `1.9.0-dev`
**Stories:** [GOV-510](GOV-510-twin-hub-contact-convergence-audit.md) and [GOV-511](GOV-511-d-tier-fifth-space-census.md)

## Problem statement

The project has source-backed planning observations for T1 seams and fifth-space
structure, but no field-wide, schema-closed basis for deciding whether those
observations converge into a further operator or remain bounded evidence.

## Goal

Produce outcome-honest, reproducible research artifacts for the twin-hub contact
hypothesis and a 462-record fifth-space census without turning either result into
topology, admission, runtime, or physical authority.

## Scope

**In:** definitions, source-bound datasets, deterministic validators, negative
controls, and explicit confirm/refute/partial verdicts.

**Out:** automatic EPIC-520 activation, graph writes, Court changes, new
admission, new `harmonic.C_H`, or a claim that planning evidence is canonical.

## Success criteria

1. The T1/hub/ring-midpoint hypothesis has a declared method and a result that
   closes whether it confirms, refutes, or remains partial.
2. The fifth-space census covers all 462 canonical state records under a strict
   schema and preserves source provenance.
3. GOV-512 can make its Research Gate 3 decision from completed artifacts only.

## Sequencing

```text
GOV-510 ----+
             +-> GOV-512 (Research Gate 3)
GOV-511 ----+
```

GOV-510 and GOV-511 run in parallel in Sprint 2. Their results are evidence,
not permission to open a new operator workstream. If their completed results
are recorded in `provenance/DECISION_LEDGER.md`, batch the resulting
shadow-ladder rebuild after the final Sprint 2 ledger edit; the refreshed
planning-evidence artifact is required before GOV-512 consumes the inputs.

## Definition of done

Both research stories publish deterministic evidence and a clear authority
boundary; GOV-512 receives complete inputs without relying on planning prose.

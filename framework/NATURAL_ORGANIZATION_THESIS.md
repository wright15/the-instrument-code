### Carey Coherence, Sameness, and the Quintessential Carrier

Norman Carey defines two normalized measures of scalar complexity. Let $D(S)$ be the number of **differences** in a scale, where intervals of the same generic span have different specific sizes. Let $F(S)$ be the number of **coherence failures**, consisting of ambiguities and contradictions between generic interval ordering and specific interval size. Then:

$$SQ(S)=1-\frac{D(S)}{\max D(N)}$$

and

$$CQ(S)=1-\frac{F(S)}{\max F(N)}.$$

These are formal harmonic measurements. They are not equivalent to the framework's compression coordinate $\kappa$, semantic coherence, or Mercury's operational coherence.

Carey's generated-scale construction with generator $u=7/12$ yields, at cardinality five,

$$S_{7/12}^{(5)}=\{0,2,4,7,9\},$$

the canonical Forte 5–35 pentatonic seed used by the Court. This cardinality is a full-convergent member of the hierarchy and is coherent in Carey's technical sense:

$$\boxed{CQ(5\text{–}35)=1}.$$

For well-formed scales Carey gives:

$$SQ=\frac{2(N-2)}{3(N-1)},$$

which yields:

$$\boxed{SQ(5\text{–}35)=\frac12}.$$

The conjunction is structurally important. The canonical Court is not maximally uniform; it preserves intervallic differentiation while containing no generic-to-specific ordering failures. The framework therefore reads 5–35 as a **coherent but differentiated carrier**: Quintessence coordinates difference without requiring sameness. This interpretive sentence belongs to the framework; the CQ and SQ values remain independent mathematical properties.

The same $7/12$ generator hierarchy later yields the seven-note diatonic system. Thus 5–35 and 7–35 are related not only by complementarity in the twelve-tone field, but also by membership in the same generated hierarchy at different cardinalities.

Carey treats a scale as equivalent to its modes and inversions for this analysis. CQ and SQ should therefore be stored as **family-and-tuning properties**, not as unique scores for Sun/Lydian, Moon/Ionian, and the other rooted Governors. A nodal shift within 7–35 does not create a new Carey CQ merely because the tonic changes; a translocation to another scale family may alter the scalar-complexity profile.

**Reference:** Norman Carey, “Coherence and sameness in well-formed and pairwise well-formed scales,” *Journal of Mathematics and Music* 1(2), 2007, 79–98. DOI: 10.1080/17459730701376743.

A pentatonic Court may be treated as a selective observation of a heptatonic state. For a concrete Court mask $c\in\{0,1\}^{12}$, one convenient linear implementation is the diagonal projection

$$P_c=\operatorname{diag}(c),\qquad y=P_cx.$$

$$7\text{–}35\rightarrow5\text{–}23\rightarrow7\text{–}32$$

$$P_cT\stackrel{?}{=}TP_c.$$

A failure to commute is not automatically an error; it may be exactly what distinguishes two legal Court routes. Linear-algebraic terms such as *kernel* should be used only when the chosen projection implementation is actually linear.

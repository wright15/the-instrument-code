The research hypothesis is structural correspondence among these coordinates, not numerical identity:

### Carey Coherence & Sameness of the Canonical Court

The canonical 5–35 Court has an additional formal property from Norman Carey's scale-complexity framework. Under the 12-tone equal-tempered `7/12` generator, the five-note generated scale is:

$$S_{7/12}^{(5)}=\{0,2,4,7,9\},$$

which is the canonical 5–35 Court seed used above. In Carey's terminology, a scale's **coherence failures** are ambiguities or contradictions between generic interval span and specific interval size. The normalized Coherence Quotient is:

$$CQ(S)=1-\frac{F(S)}{\max F(N)},$$

where $F(S)$ is the number of coherence failures. Because this five-note scale occurs at a full convergent of the `7/12` generator, it is coherent in Carey's sense:

$$\boxed{CQ(5\text{–}35)=1}.$$

Carey's Sameness Quotient is:

$$SQ(S)=1-\frac{D(S)}{\max D(N)},$$

where $D(S)$ counts differences between intervals of equal generic span but unequal specific size. For a well-formed scale,

$$SQ=\frac{2(N-2)}{3(N-1)},$$

so for $N=5$:

$$\boxed{SQ(5\text{–}35)=\frac12}.$$

Framework interpretation: 5–35 supplies a carrier with **perfect Carey coherence without maximal sameness**. The Court preserves differentiated interval structure while introducing no generic-to-specific interval-order conflict. This supports the canonical Quintessential role as **difference coordinated without contradiction**, but the interpretation remains framework-authored; the CQ and SQ values themselves are formal harmonic measurements.

The same `7/12` generator hierarchy also produces the seven-note diatonic system. This gives the canonical 5–35 Court and canonical 7–35 Governor field a second formal relationship in addition to set-class complementation: they belong to the same generated-scale hierarchy at cardinalities five and seven.

**Source:** Norman Carey, “Coherence and sameness in well-formed and pairwise well-formed scales,” *Journal of Mathematics and Music* 1(2), 2007, 79–98. DOI: 10.1080/17459730701376743.

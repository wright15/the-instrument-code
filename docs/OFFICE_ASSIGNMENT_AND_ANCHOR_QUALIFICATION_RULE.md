# Office Assignment and Anchor Qualification Rule

## 1. Eligible relation

An office claim begins only from an eligible Hamming-2 relation in one of the
two declared channels:

- fixed-tonic single-degree mutation;
- adjacent-root phase displacement.

The channels are never collapsed. Phase evidence can corroborate a seat or
close a modal seam, but it does not retroactively become a fixed-tonic midpoint.

## 2. Tier precedence

Evaluate the highest established tier first:

`A0 → A1 → A2 → D1 → D2 → D3 → D4 → D5 → D6 → D7`

Once an earlier tier has assigned a state, later tiers may record evidence but
may not overwrite its role or office.

## 3. Direct satellite

At the active tier, a state is a direct satellite when:

1. it has one eligible governing parent;
2. it is not an exact bridge selected by that tier's anchor-construction rule;
3. no earlier tier has already resolved it.

The state inherits its parent's Governor office.

This is the “neatly seated” case: the office is inherited, not constructed.

## 4. Exact midpoint anchor bridge

A fixed-tonic midpoint bridge requires:

1. the candidate is Hamming distance 2 from two eligible endpoints;
2. the endpoints are Hamming distance 4 from one another;
3. the endpoint tier is the highest currently eligible tier;
4. the framework declares a deterministic relational office for that endpoint
   configuration.

The bridge becomes an anchor and receives the declared relational office. It
does not inherit either endpoint office by plurality.

Examples already present in the framework include the A1 and A2 midpoint
constructions.

## 5. Same-office convergence anchor

Later derived tiers can qualify through same-office convergence rather than an
endpoint midpoint. A candidate family may be promoted only when the audit
declares and verifies:

1. a complete seven-mode orbit;
2. no earlier direct claim;
3. a uniform contact signature for every mode;
4. independent established contacts that agree on one office;
5. exactly one mode in each of the seven Governor offices;
6. fixed/phase closure across the full orbit;
7. any tier-specific symmetry or partner requirement;
8. no unresolved contradiction under precedence.

D7 adds the terminal condition that no residual child state remains.

This is the mechanism used for the derived D tiers, including the 7-1 / D7
terminal ring.

## 6. Chiral convergence limitation

Same-office contact agreement is necessary but not sufficient for promotion.
When a chiral family has two orientation-dependent rings, its contact office is
recorded as `relationalOffice`, while categorical `office` remains null unless
the framework declares an orientation-resolution operator.

Therefore:

- 7-1 can become D7 because it is an achiral, office-complete terminal orbit;
- 7-Z36, 7-23, 7-Z18, 7-9, 7-4, and 7-3 remain oriented convergence rings;
- stable multi-office families remain junctions;
- one-contact families remain peripheral.

## 7. Decision sequence

For each unresolved state or complete candidate orbit:

1. apply precedence;
2. evaluate fixed-tonic relations;
3. evaluate adjacent-root phase relations separately;
4. test declared midpoint construction;
5. test direct satellite inheritance;
6. test any declared same-office convergence-anchor rule;
7. otherwise assign a typed boundary role and leave the Governor office null.

This sequence preserves the distinction between office inheritance, anchor
construction, relational evidence, and unresolved topology.

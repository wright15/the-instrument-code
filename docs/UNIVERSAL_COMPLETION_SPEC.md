# Universal Heptatonic Completion Specification

## Scope

This completion adds the full rooted seven-note universe beneath the validated
Seven Governors network:

- 462 rooted pitch-class states;
- 66 transpositional/modal orientations;
- 38 Forte Tn/I classes;
- 10 achiral families and 28 chiral families.

The prior 399-state A0–D6 graph is preserved. Sixty-three missing states are
added: seven become the validated 7-1 D7 terminal anchors and fifty-six complete
the typed chiral boundary field.

state into a Governor-office member. `relationalOffice` and

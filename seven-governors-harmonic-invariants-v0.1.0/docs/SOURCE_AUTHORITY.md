## Carey evaluation

Formal source:

> Norman Carey. "Coherence and sameness in well-formed and pairwise
> well-formed scales." Journal of Mathematics and Music 1(2), 79-98, 2007.
> DOI `10.1080/17459730701376743`.

The implementation uses exact directed interval instances `(generic, origin,
destination, specific)`. Differences compare equal generic spans. Ambiguities
and contradictions compare lower and higher generic spans. For 5-35 it
enumerates `D=20`, zero ambiguities, and zero contradictions. The Carey
cardinality normalizers are generated as 40 difference slots and 25 failure
slots; no floating point is used.

The canonical name is **Sameness Quotient**. The frozen profile registry's
"simplicity quotient" wording is a recorded terminology defect and is not
edited in place.

## Aggregate C_H

Court geometry, Carey CQ/SQ, and the structured heptatonic coordinates do not
define an aggregate harmonic compression formula. `harmonic.C_H` therefore
remains `status: unresolved`, `value: null`, and explicitly distinct from
`C_P`, `C_S`, `kappa_court`, and thermodynamic quantities.

The active profile registry exposes three distinct coordinate concepts. This
companion also proposes a fourth, Fivefold/Court coordinate. They must never be
collapsed into one or assigned a stronger epistemic status than their owning
artifact supports.

## Photonic compression $C_P$

Starting with the declared representative wavelength:

$$
\nu=\frac{c}{\lambda},
\qquad
E_\gamma=h\nu=\frac{hc}{\lambda}.
$$

The active profile registry normalizes inverse wavelength across the seven
declared anchors. Sun and Saturn being local coordinate endpoints is a registry
convention, not an absolute physical statement about all light.

Rules:

- store wavelength with units;
- derive frequency and energy deterministically;
- record constants and calculation version;
- resolve an alternative state through its State Governor;
- never alter wavelength because a musical degree changed; and
- never use $C_P$ as a semantic intensity score.

## Harmonic compression $C_H$

$C_H$ deliberately remains unresolved. Candidate observables include:

- anchor precedence/depth;
- fixed-tonic and phase contact signatures;
- Hamming neighborhoods;
- orbit structure and stabilizers;
- chirality/orientation;
- Carey CQ/SQ under declared tuning;
- graph centrality;
- operator-domain size;
- confluence density; and
- spectral/Fourier features of the pitch mask.

These are a feature vector, not yet a scalar formula. The next mathematically
responsible step is to test candidate measures against named invariants and
counterexamples, then decide whether one scalar is useful at all.

## Semantic compression $C_S$

The canonical order is:

1. Sun — emission
2. Moon — reception
3. Mars — activation
4. Mercury — transduction
5. Jupiter — distribution
6. Venus — coupling
7. Saturn — fixation

A normalized ordinal may be computed as:

$$
C_S(g_i)=\frac{i}{6},\quad i\in\{0,\ldots,6\}.
$$

The spacing is conventional and nonmetric. The difference between emission
and reception is not a measured amount equal to the difference between
coupling and fixation.

## Candidate Court internalization $\kappa_{\text{Court}}$

For candidate Court state `Ci`:

$$
\kappa_{\text{Court}}(C_i)=\frac{i}{4}.
$$

This is an exact coordinate on this companion's declared five-state candidate
path. It is not an active runtime value, a thermodynamic state variable, or a
replacement for $C_S$: `C_S` belongs to an admitted Governor profile process,
while proposed Court compression would belong to a separately admitted
production context.

## Bridge policy

A bridge between coordinates must declare:

- source and target types;
- mathematical function;
- domain and units;
- epistemic status;
- positive fixtures;
- counterexamples or failure boundary; and
- whether it is descriptive, predictive, or causal.

Until then, cross-coordinate observations remain hypotheses. The installed
release does not expose the proposed Mercury/Virgo ledger service.
